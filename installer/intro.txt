Welcome to 3LCS — Lifecycle Services Companion

3LCS is a free, open-source desktop client for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Lifecycle Services (LCS). It gives you fast access to your environments, packages, diagnostics, and more — all from your Windows taskbar.

What gets installed
  • 3LCS application files  →  %LOCALAPPDATA%\3LCS
  • Start Menu shortcut

No administrator rights are required. The installation is per-user only and will not affect other accounts on this machine.

Data stored on your machine
  3LCS stores your LCS session cookie in %APPDATA%\3LCS to keep you authenticated between sessions. This data never leaves your machine and is not shared with any third party. You can clear it at any time from within the application, or by uninstalling 3LCS (the uninstaller removes the %APPDATA%\3LCS folder automatically).

After installation, 3LCS will check for updates automatically each time it starts. You will be prompted before any download begins.
